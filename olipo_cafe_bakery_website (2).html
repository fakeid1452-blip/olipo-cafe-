Welcome to Olipo

Your Neighborhood Warm Spot in Duggal Colony

Located conveniently in Pocket A, Duggal Colony, Khanpur, Olipo Cafe & Bakery was built around a simple passion: serving exceptionally crafted food with genuine warm hospitality.

Whether you are a freelancer looking for a cozy laptop-friendly corner, students taking a study break, or a family craving rich white sauce pasta, loaded Biscoff waffles, and wood-fired style pizzas — we have created the perfect space for you.
